### Course1 Module 1
### Supply Chain Logistics

__Motor Freight(汽车货运)__
1. Speed
2. Reliability(Trucks rarely break down)
3. Low Damage to the items
4. Accessibiliy(Truck can get to anywhere almost)

__Truck Types__
1. City Trucks(小卡车，无拖挂)
2. Line Haul-Vehicles(大货车，有拖挂trailer)
3. Specialty Vehicles(冷链车/运送活物的/运车的)

__Terimals(中转站)__
> Taking products from one truck, sorting them and then moving them out on a different truck
> 指货物不入库或短暂停留
> 从 **入站卡车（inbound truck）** 卸货
> 进行分拣（sorting / consolidation）
> 再装到出站卡车（outbound truck）

__3 Types of Terminals__
__1. Pickup/Delivery__
> Handles local pickup and local delivery
> Interfaces directly with customers
> Acts as the first-mile and last-mile terminal
> Freight typically starts or ends its journey here
> 负责本地提货（Pickup）和本地派送（Delivery）
> 直接面对客户（工厂、仓库、零售商）
> 是货物运输的起点或终点

__2. Cross Docks__
> Freight is unloaded, sorted, and reloaded
> Minimal or no storage
> Focused on consolidation(合并) and redistribution(分配)
> High throughput(吞吐量), fast turnaround
> 货物从一辆卡车卸下
> 进行分拣、合并或拆分
> 不入库或短暂停留
> 直接装到另一辆卡车继续运输

__3. Relay(接力转运站)__
> Used for long-haul transportation
> Drivers or tractors are swapped
> Freight usually stays on the same trailer
> Ensures hours-of-service compliance
> 用于长途运输
> 不进行分拣、不拆货
> 只换司机或牵引车
> 拖车本身不卸货
> 主要目的是满足司机工时法规

__3 Basic Form of Operation__
__1. Truckload(整车运输)(TL)__
> Truckload (TL) refers to a shipment that fills an entire truck or is dedicated to a single shipper, moving directly from origin to destination with no intermediate handling.
> 一整辆卡车只装一个客户的货
> 不与其他客户拼货
> 点对点直达（Origin → Destination）
> 途中不分拣、不转运

__2. Less-than-Truckload(LTL)(拼车运输)__
> Less-than-Truckload (LTL) is a transportation mode where multiple shippers share the same truck, and freight is picked up, sorted at terminals, and delivered through a hub-and-spoke network.
> 多个客户的货物拼在同一辆卡车
> 每个客户货量不足一整车
> 货物需要经过转运中心（terminals）
> 通过 “提货 → 分拣 → 干线 → 再分拣 → 派送” 完成运输

__3. Parcel(包裹运输)__
> Parcel shipping refers to the transportation of small, individual packages handled through highly automated hub-and-spoke networks, typically delivered by integrated carriers.
> 单件或少量包裹
> 尺寸、重量较小
> 高度标准化
> 通过自动分拣中心运输
> 由 快递公司全链路承运

__Air Freight__
1. Speed
2. Capacity
3. Accessibility

__2 Basic types of operation__
__1. All-Cargo(全货机运营)__
> All-Cargo operations use aircraft that are dedicated entirely to freight, with no passengers on board.
> 飞机只运货，不载客
> 机舱全部用于货物
> 航线、时刻为货运专门设计
> 适合大宗、重型或特殊货物

__2. Commercial Airline__
> Commercial Airline cargo operations carry freight in the belly hold of passenger aircraft, alongside passenger luggage.
> 利用 **客机腹舱（Belly Cargo）** 运货
> 货物与乘客行李共用空间
> 以客运航线为主，货运为辅

__Intermodal Transportation(多式联运)__
> Intermodal transportation refers to moving cargo using two or more different modes of transport (truck, rail, ship, air) under a single contract, typically without handling the freight itself when changing modes.
> 使用 两种或以上运输方式（如卡车 + 火车 + 船 + 飞机）运输货物
> 在换运输方式时 货物无需重新装卸
> 通过 统一合同 / 统一单证 管理运输
> 常用于 长距离、大宗货物

### Module 2
### Warehousing and Inventory Mangement仓储和库存管理
__Warehouse Advantages__
__1. Efficiencies__
> Warehouses allow companies to hold inventory where it’s needed and just as much as needed, which helps reduce overstock and improves responsiveness.
> 仓库可以将库存放在最需要的地方，并保持适量库存，提高供应链效率，避免库存过多或缺货。

__2. Risk Pooling(风险共担)__
> By holding inventory in one or fewer locations, companies can reduce variability and risk—for example, if demand is uncertain, centralizing inventory lowers the total safety stock needed.
> 通过把库存集中在一个或少数几个仓库，可以降低需求波动带来的风险，减少整体安全库存，提高库存管理效率。
> 当你在多个地点各自持有库存时，每个地点都需要独立准备安全库存以应对当地需求波动。

> 当你把库存集中在一个或少数几个仓库时，整体需求波动会被“平均化”，不需要每个仓库都各自备大量安全库存。

__3. Service__
> Warehouses improve customer service by enabling faster order fulfillment and higher product availability.
> 仓库能够提升服务水平，让客户更快收到产品，同时提高产品可得性（库存满足率）。

__3 Basic Functions of Warehouse__
__1. Movement__
> Warehouses facilitate the movement of goods—receiving from suppliers, transferring within the facility, and shipping to customers or other locations.
> 仓库负责货物的搬运和流转，包括收货、内部转运以及发货到客户或其他仓库。

__2. Storage__
> Warehouses provide a place to store inventory until it is needed, helping companies balance supply and demand.
> 仓库提供库存存储空间，使企业能够在供应与需求之间保持平衡。

__3. Information__
> Warehouses collect and manage information about inventory levels, orders, and shipments, supporting decision-making and efficient operations.
> 仓库负责库存、订单和运输信息管理，支持企业决策和提高运营效率。

__What makes a warehouse great__
__1. Speed__
> A great warehouse can process orders quickly, including receiving, picking, packing, and shipping, which reduces lead time and improves customer satisfaction.
> 优秀的仓库能够快速处理订单，包括收货、拣货、打包和发货，从而缩短交货周期，提高客户满意度。

__2. Quality/Redundamcy(质量 / 冗余保障)__
> High-quality warehouses maintain accurate inventory, minimal errors, and backup systems to prevent disruptions in operations.
> 优秀仓库能够保持库存准确、降低错误率，并有备份系统，确保运营不中断。

__3. Productivity__
> A productive warehouse uses efficient layout, technology, and labor management to maximize output while minimizing costs.
> 高效仓库通过合理布局、技术应用和劳动管理，在降低成本的同时最大化作业产出。

__Type of Inventory__

__1. Cycle Stock(循环库存)__
> Cycle stock is the portion of inventory used to satisfy regular demand between __replenishments补给__. It is replenished on a planned schedule.
> 循环库存是为了满足日常需求而保留的库存，它会按照计划周期进行补货。
> 例如：每周向供应商订货，收到后库存恢复到预定水平。
> 商店每天平均卖 50 件商品，每周补货一次：
> 循环库存 = 50 × 7 = 350 件
> 每天卖出 50 件后，库存降到 0 前补货到 350 件
> 随着销售或使用不断减少
> 定期补货恢复到预定水平
> 主要用于维持生产或销售的连续性

__2. Safety Stock(安全库存)__
> Safety stock is extra inventory held to protect against uncertainty, such as fluctuations in demand or supply delays.
> 安全库存是为了应对不确定性而额外保留的库存，例如需求波动或供应延迟。
> 防止缺货
> 提高服务水平
> 与循环库存叠加形成总库存

__Order Inventory__
__1. S.Q Method(定量订货法 / Fixed Quantity Method)__
> The S.Q (Fixed Quantity) method triggers a __replenishment补货__ order of a fixed quantity whenever inventory drops to the reorder point.
> 每次订货数量固定（例如 100 件）
> 订货时间不固定，取决于库存消耗速度
> 简单易操作，适用于需求稳定的产品

__2. S.S Method(定期订货法 / Fixed Interval Method)__
> The S.S (Fixed Interval) method places an order at regular, fixed time intervals, adjusting the order quantity to bring inventory up to a target level.
> S.S 方法是一种 定期订货法，在固定时间间隔下单，根据库存情况调整订货数量，使库存恢复到目标水平。
> 下单时间固定（如每周一）
> 订货数量可变，根据库存差额计算
> 适用于需求波动较大或供应商要求固定周期下单的情况

### Module 3
### Logistics Network

__Lead Time__
> Lead Time is the total time that elapses from the moment an order is placed until the product is received and available for use.
> Lead Time 是指从下单开始，到货物可用为止所经历的总时间。

__Fill Rate(订单满足率 / 供货满足率)__
> Fill Rate measures the percentage of customer demand that is satisfied immediately from available inventory, without backorders or delays.
> Fill Rate（订单满足率） 指客户需求中，有多少比例能够被现有库存立即满足，而不需要延期或补货。
> 为了维持较高的 Fill Rate，企业要么增加安全库存，要么缩短并稳定提前期
